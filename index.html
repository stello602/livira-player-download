GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Does LIVIRA include TV channels or movie subscriptions?

No. LIVIRA is player software. You supply playlists or credentials from services you are authorized to use. LIVIRA does not sell or provide television channels, movies, or media subscriptions.

Can I install LIVIRA on Samsung or LG Smart TVs?

Not with the Android APK. Native Samsung Tizen and LG webOS versions are planned but are not yet available.

Can one playlist work on multiple TVs?

LIVIRA's future software licensing may support multiple devices, but the content provider sets its own streaming connection rules. You should follow your provider's terms.

Are the annual plans available to purchase now?

Not yet. The displayed prices are proposals for future software activation plans. Payment and customer account features are still being developed.

Is LIVIRA available in Google Play?

Not yet. The Android test APK is available through the official LIVIRA GitHub release. Google TV / Android TV store distribution is planned.
